# PrePro v0.8.0: podcasts with two people, and faster renders on your GPU

## Two people, one camera
Film a podcast or an interview with one wide camera, and PrePro now edits it like a podcast clipper:

- **It knows who is talking.** It finds each person's seat and works out who says each line from whose mouth moves
  (nodding and bobbing don't count). When it isn't sure, it doesn't guess.
- **Shorts follow the conversation.**
  - In a 9:16 Short, the frame is on whoever is talking, and it cuts to the other person at the end of a line.
  - A quick "yeah" doesn't trigger a cut, and a short exclamation ("Eleven times!") gets a quick reaction shot.
  - The frame never sits on the empty space between the two of you.
- **Every answer stays with its question.** A conversation is cut as one stretch, in the order it was said. It
  opens on a line that stands on its own, never on a reply, and ends on a landing, not a question left hanging.
- **Every cut is your call.** Each one is listed with its reason in the review app (new "framing" filter). Reject a
  cut and the frame stays on the person already on screen.

## Faster renders when your GPU helps
PrePro now tests your PC once and renders on the graphics card (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) when that is clearly faster.
Otherwise it uses the CPU. Not every PC has a GPU, and on small previews a fast CPU often wins. If the GPU has a
problem mid-render, the CPU finishes the job. `prepro doctor` shows what it picked and the times it measured.

## Fixed
- **Captions jumped between above the head and below the chin** from one cut to the next. They now stay in one
  place for the whole video and move only where they would cover someone's mouth.
- **On-screen hook text could garble your words.** "It started with one video I almost did not upload" became
  "Started with 1 video almost did?!", which lost the "not". Hook text is now always a whole phrase that means what
  you said ("1 video I almost didn't upload?!", "Zero to 1M subscribers in 1 year?!"), and it stays up long enough
  to read.
- **A reply that repeated the other person's words** was mistaken for a second take of the same line, and the
  original was cut.
- **"Welcome back. Today I have a creator who…"** was cut whole as a greeting; now only "Welcome back." goes.
- **Your guest is never left out.** The face detector found the darker-skinned guest in our test podcast far less
  often, so finding people no longer depends only on the faces it spots easily.
- **Clean GPU-rendered versions in the phone pack** now have an exactly constant frame rate, as phone editors
  require.

## Verified here
On Windows 11 (i9-14900HX, RTX 4070, Intel UHD), every engine test file was run one at a time:
- **The two-person test podcast:** 11 of 11 lines got the right speaker. The Short frames the speaker in every shot
  and scores 10/10 on the cringe guard, and all 40 captions sit in the same place.
- **GPU vs CPU on a 32-second cut:**
  - previews: "auto" chose the CPU (10.8 s; NVENC took 12.3 s, Quick Sync 17.1 s)
  - 1080p renders: "auto" chose NVENC (18.1 s, against 21.8 s on the CPU)
  - the GPU picture matches the CPU's (SSIM 0.993)
- Real footage end to end with Whisper speech recognition.
