# PrePro v0.5.0: packaging and Style DNA

The cut is only half the job; the other half is what people see before they tap. PrePro now prepares that too,
from the same footage and the same hook.

## Packaging
- **Three thumbnail picks**, each a different kind:
  - a big, clear face
  - the peak action moment
  - a striking shot

  Each comes as a ready mock-up at the right size (1280×720 for YouTube, 1080×1920 cover for Shorts/Reels). Bold
  text sits away from the face, built around the number in your hook: "₹5000 for 3 days", "1M views phone camera?".
- **Title options** that keep your video's actual promise (and its numbers):
  - "How I lost $10,000 on my first channel"
  - "The real reason some videos get 1M views from a phone camera"
  - "Stop making these 3 editing mistakes"
  - "Calangute → Jaipur on ₹5,000?"
- **YouTube chapters** that follow YouTube's rules (start at 0:00, at least three, each at least 10 s), named
  after your day/place cards. When a video can't have chapters, PrePro says why.
- **Description with hashtags**, ready to paste.

Open the Review step → **More** → **Make thumbnails & titles**, or tick **Packaging** when exporting. The files
go to `PrePro_exports/<cut>/packaging/`.

## Style DNA: "cut it like the videos I love"
Put 1–5 reference videos into a `style/` folder in your shoot, then open the **Brief** step → **Style DNA** →
**Measure**. PrePro measures how they are edited:
- how often they cut
- how long the shots are
- how much is talking and how tight the pauses are
- whether music leads or sits under the voice
- how much of the time text is on screen
- how long they are

Your next cut follows that style. Your own settings and brief always win, and the reference videos are never put
into your edit or uploaded anywhere.
