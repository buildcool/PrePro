# PrePro v0.12.2: changing the song re-cuts the reel on the new song

## "Change the song" on a photo reel, a day in my life or your words on video
Asking for another song used to swap the track and keep everything else from the old one:
- the point where the cut started in it, so the new song could start mid-phrase, or run out and start again in the
  middle of your reel;
- how many beats each picture held, so a faster song suddenly cut much quicker.

Now PrePro re-cuts the reel on the new song, as if your brief had named it:
- it starts on the new song's own strongest part (its drop or chorus);
- every photo or moment is laid on its beats again, at a pace that suits its tempo;
- your lines land on its beats.

The photos you dropped, the cover you picked and your other review choices all stay. Say "change the song" again to
hear the songs you haven't tried yet, and then the one you had.

## The song never runs out halfway
A short song (a 20-second sound, say) used to run out and start again from the top in the middle of your day. Now:
- the cut ends with the song;
- a day in my life keeps the best moment of each part of the day, and the rest wait in the review if you want them back;
- your words on video keep every line and start where the most song is left.

## The cover's place and date are readable on a fast song
On a fast song the cover could flash by too quickly to read "Goa · Oct 2026". Any words on a photo now keep it on
screen until they can be read.

The desktop app updates itself to this version (or asks first, depending on Settings → Updates).
