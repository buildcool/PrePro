# PrePro v0.11.0: Day in my life

## Your day, cut in the order it happened
Drop in a day of phone clips (coffee at 7, the commute, lunch, the beach at sunset, the night out) and PrePro
recognises it and cuts a **Day in my life** Reel:

- **In time order**, from each clip's own capture time.
- **On the beat of a light song.** Every moment lasts a few beats (about a second and a half). The first and last
  moments of the day linger. The song plays through to the end without a seam.
- **The whole day, not just the morning.** When it doesn't all fit, the best moment of each part of the day stays
  in. Anything left out says why, and you can bring it back. It goes back where it happened, still on the beat.
- **"a day in my life" on the first frame**, or your own line if your brief has one.
- **Small time stamps** like "6:42 am" where your day starts, whenever it moves on and where it ends. They don't go
  on every clip, so it doesn't read like a ticking clock. One click in the review turns them off.
- **The sounds of your day** (a pan sizzling, typing, waves, friends laughing) stay under the music.

The times are the ones your phone showed: an iPhone's local time, a photo's EXIF time, or for Android videos the
time zone your other files state. A day runs from 4 am to 4 am, so the 1 am ride home belongs to the night before.

## Better with an AI editor connected
Photo reels and days now stay on the beat when an AI editor is connected. Before, the AI could reorder the moments
and change the song's start, which pulled the cuts off the beat. The AI still helps choose the direction and
describe your shots.

## Also
- Two look-alike shots count as a retake only when shot within half an hour. The same room in the morning and at
  night are two moments.
- A wordless cut that keeps its natural sound is no longer flagged for "music too loud under the voice".

The desktop app updates itself to this version (or asks first, depending on Settings → Updates).
