# PrePro v0.12.0: your words on video

## POV and text-on-video Reels
Write your lines in the brief, for example:

```
Text:
pov: you finally learned to cook
and it's actually good
sunday mornings hit different now
save this for your next slow weekend
```

Add a few clips and a song, and PrePro makes the Reel:
- **Your first line opens on your most striking clip**, from the very first frame. That's your hook.
- **Every next line arrives with a cut on the beat** and stays up until it can be read. A long line runs over two
  clips, and the cut changes under it on the beat.
- **Your words exactly as you wrote them**, one line at a time, centred and moved clear of faces.
- **The last line holds a little longer**, and the song plays through to the end.

If your clips are a whole day, the day-in-my-life cut comes first and your lines go on it. Photos still get a photo
reel with your lines on them.

## Also
- An AI editor keeps this format on the beat, the same as photo reels and days.

The desktop app updates itself to this version (or asks first, depending on Settings → Updates).
