# PrePro v0.12.1: Gemini works again, and you can see who's signed in

## Gemini, through Google's Antigravity CLI
On 18 June 2026 Google stopped Gemini CLI for personal Google accounts, so signing in failed with "This client is no
longer supported for Gemini Code Assist for individuals". PrePro's Gemini option now uses Google's replacement, the
**Antigravity CLI**:
- **Settings → AI editor → Gemini → Install** runs Google's own installer (no Node.js needed).
- **Sign in** once with your Google account. Free, Google AI Pro and Ultra accounts all work.
- **Test**, then **Use as AI editor**.

If you had chosen Gemini before, PrePro moves you over on its own. Google doesn't document a way to send pictures to
Antigravity yet, so with Gemini the AI editor works from shot descriptions and skips the small thumbnails.

## "Signed in as …"
Each plan card now says who it's signed in as (for Claude, your email and Claude Pro or Max). The sidebar shows it
next to "Your plan". If a tool says it isn't signed in, the card tells you and Sign in is the main button. PrePro gets
this from each tool's own status command and never opens a login file.

## A broken account stays out of the way
An account whose last test failed isn't offered at the top any more. Working ones come first, a failing one goes to
the bottom of its list, and if it's your current AI editor it says "Not working — fix it below".

The desktop app updates itself to this version (or asks first, depending on Settings → Updates).
