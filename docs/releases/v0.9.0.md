# PrePro v0.9.0: photo reels, and the desktop app starts again on Windows

## Important: if v0.8.0 didn't start
On some Windows PCs the v0.8.0 desktop app stopped at "Could not start PrePro: engine exited during start-up". Its
first line of output contained a "→", which the Windows code page couldn't encode. v0.9.0 fixes this, and every
build now starts the engine exactly the way the app does before it is packaged.

## Photo reels
**A folder of photos and a song becomes a photo-dump Reel.**
- It's cut on the song's beat, about 1.2 s a photo, starting at the song's chorus or drop, on its bar line.
- Photos play in the order you took them (from the photo's own capture time, not the file name).
- It opens on a cover: your most striking photo of a person, one that fills the 9:16 frame.
- It ends back on the cover, continuing the cover's own move, so the Reel replays without a seam.
- With no words of yours, the cover gets where and when from your photos ("Calangute · Oct 2026").

**One photo, a few lines and a song.** Put your lines in the brief:

```
Text:
POV: you finally took the trip you kept postponing
3 days, 1 backpack, zero plans
and it was everything
```

Your first line is on screen from the first frame (it's the hook). Each next line arrives on the beat and stays up
long enough to read, and the last one holds to the end. Your words appear exactly as you wrote them.

**Photos are handled like an editor would.**
- Sideways phone photos are turned upright.
- Burst duplicates, blurry and dark photos are left out, each with its reason. A portrait-mode photo with a soft
  background isn't "blurry".
- Photos move like a camera: a slow push-in or a gentle drift, drawn smoothly instead of stepping.
- Nobody is cut out of a group photo. When everyone won't fit a 9:16 crop, the photo is shown whole, like a print,
  on a soft wash of its own colours.
- Words never sit on a face. They go above the head or below the chin, and are set a little smaller on a tight
  close-up.

**Every decision is yours.** In the review app you can bring back any photo that was left out, ask for another
cover, or turn off the loop, the motion, the title or any line. However you change the reel, every cut stays on the
beat and your lines move with their photos.

## Fixed for every video
- **Bar lines were two beats off** in songs whose kick plays on beats 1 and 3, so music-led cuts could land mid-bar.
- **Cuts could drift off the beat** over a long video (each piece was rounded to whole frames on its own).
- **Loudness:** the limiter now also catches peaks between samples, so −1 dBTP really holds, and a dense music-only
  mix still reaches −14 LUFS.
- **Gaming Shorts** (on top of v0.8.0): the webcam stacked over the game, big on the biggest win, fail memes, GIF
  memes render.
