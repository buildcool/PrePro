# PrePro v0.7.0: the gaming pack, and PrePro on Windows for real

Gaming footage is now edited the way gaming editors do it, around the plays rather than the talking. And this is
the first release checked end to end on a Windows PC, which found and fixed several Windows-only problems.

- **PrePro hears the plays.** It spots explosions, shots and hits in the game audio and pairs each one with your
  reaction ("Double kill! Let's go!"). Your voice starting to talk is never mistaken for a hit.
- **A highlight cut that works like a highlight video:**
  1. It opens on the best play.
  2. Each play comes with about 3 seconds of build-up into it. Your commentary stays at full volume and is
     captioned. Dead gameplay in between is cut.
  3. The best play is replayed in slow motion and labelled REPLAY.
  4. It ends on your last play, for example the victory.
- **A callout in your own words** (for example "DOUBLE KILL!") lands as you say it.
- **"Open on the best play"** is the top hook on the gaming direction card.

## Windows
- **Speech recognition works on Windows.**
  - The offline fallback recogniser couldn't load on Windows; it can now.
  - If speech can't be recognised at all, PrePro tells you why and how to fix it, instead of cutting your talking
    clips blind.
- **Your GPU is used when it can be.** On PCs with an NVIDIA graphics card but without NVIDIA's CUDA libraries
  (most gaming PCs), speech recognition now runs on the CPU, instead of quietly falling back to the rough
  recogniser. `prepro doctor` shows which one is used and why.
- **Claude Code and Codex as your AI editor work on Windows**, including when installed with npm. For Claude Code,
  the native installer is recommended: `winget install Anthropic.ClaudeCode`.

## Fixed
- A fix for dull stretches could speed up gameplay (and mute it). Speed-ups now only touch silent B-roll, and
  moving footage is never treated as dull.
- v0.6.0 had no Linux installers because one download failed during the build. The build now retries and has a
  second, checksum-verified source.

## Verified here
- On Windows 11 (i9-14900HX, RTX 4070 without CUDA libraries), every engine test file (unit and end to end) was
  run one at a time, with espeak-ng. This includes the slow rendering tests and the Shotcut (`melt`) render of the
  MLT export. The browser test of the review app and the desktop-app smoke test were not run on Windows.
- Real footage end to end (Intel/OpenCV sample clips, JFK's speech, Kevin MacLeod music): analysis, a rendered
  cut at −14 LUFS, and the FCP7/OTIO/MLT exports.
- **Speech on real footage (JFK, 11 s), word error rate:**
  - Whisper small, on the CPU: 0 %, transcribed in 9.8 s including loading the model.
  - Offline fallback recogniser: 50 %. That is why Whisper is worth the one-time download.
- Gaming, on a recording with planted plays:
  - both plays are in the cut, with their build-up
  - the cold open has sound
  - there is exactly one 0.5× replay, and the cut ends on the payoff
  - the callout and captions are present
  - the cringe guard scores it 10/10
- The impact detector found exactly the planted explosions, and nothing in talking, podcast or comedy footage.
