# PrePro v0.10.1: the real logos of the AI you connect

## See who edits at a glance
Settings → **AI editor** now shows the real logos of **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, **Gemini**, Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI
(Grok), OpenRouter and Ollama on every account, in the "Who edits" picker and in the sidebar. Before, these were
plain letter tiles. Logos that are a single colour follow light and dark mode; Claude and Gemini keep their own colours.

The logos are trademarks of their owners and are shown only to name the service you connect. PrePro isn't
affiliated with any of them. The logo files are LobeHub lobe-icons (MIT); the licence ships with the app.

## Fixed
- The installed tool's version reads cleanly ("Through Claude Code 2.1.318") and no longer disappears from the card
  after you click something.
- In a narrow window the sidebar no longer squeezes the AI chip and the Settings button.

The desktop app updates itself to this version (or asks first, depending on Settings → Updates).
