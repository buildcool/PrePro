# PrePro v0.10.0: one click to a finished cut, your AI connected, and a new look

## One click, about 90 % of the edit
Choose your shoot folder and press **Make my first cut**. PrePro:
1. watches and listens to every clip,
2. finds the story (the best direction for this footage),
3. edits,
4. renders a preview,
5. prepares the exports (the Premiere / Resolve timeline, OTIO, captions and the edit report).

The review then opens on its own, with the video playing. Opening a new shoot starts all this straight away (you can
turn that off in Settings → General). Your brief, the directions and your style references are now ways to refine the
cut, not steps you have to take first. Every decision is still explained, and you can undo any of them.

## Progress you can actually follow
A progress card says what PrePro is doing in plain words ("Finding the beat in warm_acoustic_92.wav", "Cutting piece
3 of 9", "Mixing the sound"). It shows a stepper for the whole cut, elapsed time and an estimate of time left, and it
never looks frozen. **Stop** ends any task without touching what you had.

## Connect the AI you already use
Settings → **AI editor**:
- **Your subscription:** Claude Pro or Max (through Claude Code), ChatGPT Plus or Pro (through the Codex CLI), or
  Gemini with Google AI Pro or a Google account (through the Gemini CLI). Install and sign-in open a terminal window
  running the provider's own command; your login stays with them.
- **Your own API key:** Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI or OpenRouter. Keys are saved in your computer's
  keychain and never shown again.
- Whatever you connect becomes your **AI editor**, and every editing task uses it. The sidebar always shows who
  edits. With nothing connected, PrePro still edits with its free, offline rules engine.

Your footage never leaves your computer. Only text, and small thumbnails if you allow them, goes to the AI you choose.

## A redesigned app
- A desktop-style layout with a sidebar.
- A real **Settings** window: General, AI editor, Privacy, Speech recognition, Rendering, Updates and About.
- **Light, dark, or match my computer.**
- A new home page with your recent shoots shown as covers.
