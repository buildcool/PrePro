# PrePro v0.6.0: Editor's Craft v2

This release adds the things that make a cut feel *edited* rather than stitched together. Every technique below
was taken from how professional editors work. Each one shows up in the review app with its reason, and you can
switch each one off.

## What's new in your cuts
- **Sound and picture overlap at the right moments (J/L cuts).**
  - Your voice starts just before we cut back to you.
  - Or it keeps going as the next shot appears.
  - Or a new place's sound arrives a moment before its picture.

  Words are never clipped.
- **A reason to keep watching.**
  - **Flash-forward:** a 1-second glimpse of the best moment right after the hook, with "later…" on screen.
    PrePro makes sure the video actually shows that moment later.
  - **Re-hooks:** in long talks, the next part's own question or number comes up on screen as that part begins.
- **No dull stretches.** Where nothing changes for too long, PrePro adds a matching cutaway or a punch-in on the
  key word, or speeds up a long silent shot. Calm, aesthetic videos are left calm on purpose.
- **Talking heads that don't look stitched.** The framing alternates on every jump cut, the way YouTube editors do
  it.
- **Comedy timing.**
  - Comedy skits are recognised automatically.
  - A beat before every punchline and a hold after it.
  - The meme from your folder that matches the line lands right after it.
  - The opening text teases the premise and never gives away the joke.
- **Montages that flow.** Movement keeps its direction across cuts, the same angle never appears twice in a row,
  and vertical videos end where they began, so Shorts and Reels loop.
- **Cringe guard.** A new `no cringe` score lists anything a viewer would notice:
  - empty frames
  - the same moment twice
  - effects with no reason
  - too many effects for the genre
  - music that stops dead
  - text you can't read in time
  - loudness jumps
  - memes in serious moments
- **See every path before choosing.** In **Directions**, click **Show me each path**. You get a 6-second preview of
  each option's opening, made locally for free.

## Verified here
- All 8 genre test shoots build with no errors and a `no cringe` score of 10/10.
  - The one exception is a brief that says "No zooms", where a jump cut can't be reframed; the scorecard says so.
- New tests check that:
  - split edits move only the picture
  - open loops are always paid off
  - re-hooks are readable
  - talking-head framing alternates
  - comedy keeps its beats and never plays a pause twice
  - vertical montages loop and flow
  - previews match every path
- Storyboards of the comedy skit and the travel vlog were reviewed as an editor would. Two problems were found and
  fixed before release: a text hook that spoiled the punchline, and a missing meme that was sitting in the folder.

## Research behind it (summary)
- Retention guides on pattern interrupts and open loops: limit them and always pay them off.
- Split-edit guides from Adobe, Soundstripe and MasterClass.
- Seamless-loop guides from vidIQ and CapCut.
- Gaming highlight workflows: the next release.
- Comedy-timing guides: hold the beat, cut fast to the reaction.
- Analyses of why auto-edits feel cheap: "cuts fine, rhythm wrong", "random good moments, no story",
  "clipped words".
