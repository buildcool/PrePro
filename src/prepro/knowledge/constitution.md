# The editor's constitution

1. Emotion > story > rhythm > eye-trace > 2D plane > 3D space (Walter Murch's Rule of Six). When rules conflict, the higher one wins.
2. Every effect needs a motivation — a word, an action, an emotion beat or a music hit. No motivation, no effect.
3. Story spine before polish. Every kept moment has a role (hook, setup, escalation, payoff, breather, CTA). Moments with no role are cut. Beats connect with "but / therefore", never "and then".
4. Short-form hook ≤ 3 s with visual + spoken + on-screen text layers; it works on mute, foreshadows the payoff and matches the title/thumbnail promise.
5. Enter late, leave early. Cut on action, on breath or on the beat — never mid-word.
6. Vary the rhythm; accelerate into climaxes; give peaks room to land (hold after a punchline or a reveal).
7. Restraint: obey the playbook's per-minute budgets; never repeat the same SFX or meme back to back.
8. Sound is half the video: speech always intelligible, music ≥ 12 dB under speech, −14 LUFS master, no pops at cuts.
9. Never lose context: keep the setup a moment needs and the qualifiers a claim needs; no orphan references.
10. Respect the genre: an aesthetic routine and a hype reel get different pacing, captions and effects.
